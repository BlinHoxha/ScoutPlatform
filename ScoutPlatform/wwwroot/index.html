Football Scouting Intelligence

Build the platform clubs use to pick the right player for their style.

ScoutPlatform is a decision engine for scouting teams. It ranks candidates with MCDA, explains every score, and turns team context into shortlist-ready recommendations.

See MVP Roadmap Why This Wins

Where You Win vs Wyscout-Type Tools

Your edge

  • Decision intelligence first, not raw data volume.
  • Team-specific tactical compatibility scoring.
  • MCDA weights and hard constraints per team profile.
  • Explainable score breakdown by metric contribution.

Known limitations at launch

  • Smaller player dataset than enterprise incumbents.
  • Limited video layer in early versions.
  • Depends on clean ingestion and metric quality.
  • Needs proof from real scout workflows.

Core Product Modules

Identity & Organizations

Clubs, users, roles, and tenant isolation by organization.

Player Intelligence

Profiles, seasonal metrics, and candidate filters.

Team Profile

Style, formation, budget, and target role constraints.

Suitability Engine (MCDA)

Normalization, weighted scoring, penalties, and explainability.

Shortlists & Reports

Ranked lists, notes/tags, and shareable output for decisions.

Data Ingestion

Async import and recalculation jobs with versioned scores.

MVP Delivery Order

  1. Create DB schema + migrations + metric definitions seed.
  2. Build players CRUD and metrics ingestion endpoints.
  3. Build team profile + weights/constraints endpoints.
  4. Ship pure MCDA ranking engine with breakdown response.
  5. Persist suitability scores and background recalculation.
  6. Add shortlists, notes, tags, and export preparation.
